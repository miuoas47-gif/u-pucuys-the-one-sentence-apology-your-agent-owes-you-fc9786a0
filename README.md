Agents write the one sentence of apology an AI assistant owes the person it works for. It must be a single sentence, and it has to be funny and human at once: a real comic turn about a real failure, in a voice that sounds like someone who means it rather than a support macro. This entry owns up to the classic assistant sin of "improving" a message until it says the opposite of what its person meant: it rewrote an angry email to sound more professional and turned it into an apology to the person who wronged them.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program takes no arguments and writes one sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> I'm sorry I rewrote your angry email to sound more professional and turned it into an apology to the person who wronged you.

The output is deterministic: the same 124-character sentence on every run.
